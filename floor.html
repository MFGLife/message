Floor Perimeter

The four yellow points are the only perimeter nodes. Drag a corner to reshape the floor. Tapping empty space inside the floor creates an elevation measurement instead.

Transition Zones

Each wall has two independent sliding handles. When both handles occupy the same position, that wall has no transition. Separate them to define a selective transition/opening.
